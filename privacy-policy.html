Privacy Policy — Bright Reader

Jefferson Synapps — Last updated: June 2026

About this app

Bright Reader is developed by Jefferson Synapps. This privacy policy describes how the app handles your data.

No data collection

Bright Reader does not collect any personal data and does not track users. The app does not send any information to external servers or third parties.

Local storage

The app only stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Reading progress and position in files
  • Reading speed (WPM) setting
  • Theme preference (Dark/Light/Sepia)
  • Font and text size preference
  • Reading mode preference (RSVP/Glide/Scroll/Neutral)
  • TTS language preference
  • Ambience preference

This data never leaves your device and is never shared.

Files

Files you open (EPUB, TXT, PDF) are read locally only. The content is never shared, stored outside your device, or transmitted.

Internet connection

The app does not require an internet connection. There are two exceptions:

URL import: When using the URL import feature, the requested URL is sent to a third-party text extraction service (r.jina.ai) to retrieve the page content.

Fonts: Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts CDN on first use and cached locally on your device. Google may receive your IP address during this process.

No personal data is collected or transmitted.

Device features

The app uses a wakelock to keep your screen active while reading. This is a local function with no data collection.

Your rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to view, modify or delete.

Children

This app is not intended for children under 12 years of age without supervision of an adult.
